## Description (≤ 4000 chars)

Notai is a calm note-taking app that combines the structure of a notebook
with the freedom of a whiteboard.

• Sticky notes and an infinite drawing canvas in every note.
• Quick capture from anywhere via the share sheet.
• Morning brief, daily prompts, and a streak badge that nudges, never nags.
• Tasks with due dates & recurrence, surfaced on a Today rollup.
• Hierarchical tags, cover images, and typography presets.
• Import from Notion, Evernote, and Markdown.
• Email-to-note, iCal calendar context, BYOK AI providers.
• Local-first sync — works offline, syncs when you reconnect.

Notai is privacy-first: your notes are yours. No ads, no tracking pixels, no
AI training on your data.
