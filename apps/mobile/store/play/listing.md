## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Notai is a calm, ADHD-friendly note-taking app that combines the structure
of a notebook with the freedom of a whiteboard.

• **Sticky notes & infinite canvas** — write, doodle, sketch, mind-map.
• **Quick capture** — global shortcut on desktop; share-to-Notai on mobile.
• **Daily prompts & morning brief** — ease into the day without overwhelm.
• **Tasks with due dates & recurrence** — `[ ] @due(2025-12-01) !!high`.
• **Hierarchical tags** — `#projects/notai/launch`.
• **Cover images & typography presets** (Serif / Sans / Rounded / Mono).
• **Import from Notion, Evernote, Markdown** — bring your existing notes.
• **Email-to-note** — forward an email; it becomes a note.
• **Calendar context** — subscribe to your iCal feeds; today's events
  appear on the dashboard.
• **End-to-end encrypted secrets** — your AI keys stay yours (BYOK).
• **Local-first sync** — works offline, syncs when you reconnect.

Privacy-first: your notes are yours. Self-host the open source server, or
use the hosted version. No ads, no tracking pixels, no AI training on your
data.
